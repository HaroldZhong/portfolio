# Portfolio language

The portfolio presents Harold Zhong's applied AI work and research to industry and academic visitors with equal weight.

## Language

**Selected work**: The three featured projects on the homepage. It is a curated introduction to the complete project collection.

**Projects**: The complete collection of eight project records, including Praxis AI, Scholia and BRAT, featured on the homepage. AI Advisory Board is part of Scholia's history and is not a separate record.

**Evidence label**: How a figure in a case study was measured: Internal benchmark, Human-verified golden set, or Planned evaluation. A planned evaluation is a criterion for adopting a design, never a result.

**Project card**: A project's visual introduction, containing its image or cover, title, role, status, short description, and tags.

**Case study**: The fuller account of a project's problem, contribution, approach, outcomes, and supporting material.

**Experience**: Research, teaching, professional, and community roles, including earlier roles. A role is distinct from the projects undertaken within it.

**Expertise**: The grouped descriptions of capabilities and their methods and tools, presented with skill capsules.

**Scholarly work**: Manuscripts, conference contributions, guest lectures, and invited talks. Published, under-review, and in-preparation work have distinct statuses.

**Articles**: The author's public writing, distinct from peer-reviewed publications and other scholarly records.
